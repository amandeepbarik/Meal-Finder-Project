Instructions:

Prepare the udon noodles according to the package directions.

Heat sesame oil in a wok or large pan.

Stir-fry mushrooms, cabbage, carrot and ginger until crisp-tender.

Add the udon noodles and toss well.

Add soy sauce and cook until the noodles are coated and hot.

Finish with spring onion and serve immediately.
